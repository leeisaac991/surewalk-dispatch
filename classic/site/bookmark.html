Get GroupMe token

A bookmark for SURE Walk dispatchers. Set it up once; after that, one click gets your GroupMe token for the dispatch panel.

1 · Add the bookmark (once)

Get GroupMe token
  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl ⇧ Shift B (Mac: ⌘ ⇧ Shift B).
  2. Drag the orange button above onto the bookmarks bar.

2 · Use it

  1. Open web.groupme.com (logged in).
  2. Click your Get GroupMe token bookmark. Your token is copied.
  3. Go back to the SURE Walk panel and paste it (Ctrl V) in Connect GroupMe. It saves by itself.
